Wiring rules

  1. Common ground. Every board, sensor, relay and supply shares one GND, or ADC and SPI/I²C readings fail.
  2. 3.3 V logic. ESP32 inputs take 3.3 V. Power MQ-2 / MQ-135 heaters from 5 V but divide their outputs below 3.3 V.
  3. ADC1 only with radio on. Use GPIO 32–39 for analog; ADC2 pins stop working while LoRa or Wi-Fi is active.
  4. SIM800L needs 2 A bursts. Feed it 3.7–4.2 V from an 18650 or a buck converter, never from Jetson pins.

THE PROBLEM

Why Conventional Disaster Systems Fail

Remote and disaster-prone mountain corridors, river valleys, and forests often face critical operational bottlenecks:

  • Limited Connectivity: Storms, landslides, and floods quickly sever commercial cellular towers and broadband fiber links.
  • Delayed Warnings: Traditional systems only sound sirens after a breach has already occurred, giving zero lead time.
  • Power Constraints: Remote disaster zones lack national grid power, rendering power-hungry sensor networks unusable.
  • Lack of Real-Time Monitoring: Fragmented or manual reporting creates severe blind spots during escalating events.
  • Limited Prediction Capability: Simple threshold tripwires fail to forecast rate-of-change and temporal progression.
OUR SOLUTION

What Disaster Sentinel Delivers

Disaster Sentinel provides an end-to-end, resilient infrastructure designed specifically for harsh, remote environments:

  • Real-Time Sensing: Ruggedized, solar-powered edge stations stream multi-parameter telemetry continuously.
  • Local AI-Based Prediction: On-device recurrent deep learning projects future hazard trajectories 50 minutes ahead.
  • Multi-Hazard Monitoring: Floods, landslides, wildfires, and toxic smog unified in one coordinated operations hub.
  • Early Warnings: Actionable civil defense guidance dispatched before threshold breaches claim lives.
  • Emergency Response Support: Autonomous acoustic village sirens, warning strobes, and satellite SOS dispatch.

Dual-Brain Edge AI Pipeline

Brain 1: Fast Reflex Tripwire (Random Forest)

Classifies instant sensor readings in <1 ms into Safe, Warning, or Hazardous states across all 4 hazard nodes.

Brain 2: Temporal Forecaster (PyTorch GRU)

Takes 10-step sequence windows and projects future values at +10m, +20m, +30m, +40m, +50m with lead time breach alarms.

Hardware Bus Topology & Protocols

LoRa SPI Link: SX1278 (Ra-02) Star-Mesh Hybrid (with ACK), 32-byte binary payload with CRC-16.
Sensor Buses: I2C Fast Mode (400 kHz) for BME280 (0x76) & MPU6050 (0x68). ADC1 for rain, soil, gas & flame.
Emergency Alert & GSM: SIM800L UART1 SMS dispatch, with GPIO18 Acoustic Siren & GPIO23 Optical Strobe.

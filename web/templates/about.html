Edge AI · LoRa · Multi-hazard

Warn before the limit is crossed, even with no internet.

Solar ESP32 stations stream sensor data over 433 MHz LoRa to a Jetson Orin Nano. The hub classifies each reading, forecasts 50 minutes ahead, and drives sirens, strobes and SMS on its own.
